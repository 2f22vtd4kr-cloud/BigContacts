**Part of:** APEX_ATLAS_MASTER_BUREAU_PLAN  
**Sources:** SANS Intelligence Analyst’s Playbook (BLUF, key judgments, evidence, alternatives); ACH (analysis of competing hypotheses) as a checklist habit.

## 1. Competing hypotheses on a contact

For ambiguous common names, desk/analysis should hold:

- H1: Route belongs to target at employer E  
- H2: Route belongs to different person same name  
- H3: Route is org-only  

Collision module encodes H2 risk. Outcome taxonomy encodes H3. Card promote should not “pick H1” without evidence.

## 3. Desk narration rules (RH)

- State what tool did, not what we wish were true  
- If collision risk, say so  
- Never upgrade organization_contact to personal in prose  

## 4. Apex must / must not

**Must:** alternatives/collision visible in notes or spans when risk true.  
**Must not:** single-hypothesis promote on first-name SERP hits.
