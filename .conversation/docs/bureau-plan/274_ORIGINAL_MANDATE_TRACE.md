# Volume 274 — Original Mandate Trace (Multi-Agent Research → Apex Plan)

## Purpose

This volume recovers the **starting mandate** of the architecture plan for agents and operators with short context windows: the bureau plan exists to redesign Apex research around **industry multi-agent research patterns** (especially Anthropic’s orchestrator–worker Research system), **ReAct trajectory quality**, and **honest contact outcomes**—not to accumulate micro-features.

## Mandate themes (unchanged)

1. **Orchestrator / lead vs workers** with clear task briefs and output schemas.
2. **Breadth-first multi-agent where facets are independent**; depth-first single dig per identity for contact recovery.
3. **Citation / attribution** of claims to sources.
4. **Token and effort honesty** — multi-agent is expensive; scale on purpose.
5. **Observability** of agent trajectories (spans, not theater).
6. **No dual-brain promote** — one writer policy for card columns.
7. **Free dig** — model-owned tool trajectory; no force-scripted hop lists as the product.

## What the dig-desk wave solved

Code and desk CTAs now match the mandate for **per-person contact recovery**: Dig contacts → single-target free dig → promote/rehydrate → ContactSurface → scoreboard strip. Discovery is separated as breadth. That is mandate items 2, 6, 7 made operational.

## What remains under the same mandate

1. **Live evaluation** (scoreboard milestone on a real deployment) — mandate item 5/eval honesty.
2. **COMPARE harness** with human review — external baseline, not self-grade.
3. **Citation UX polish** — mandate item 3 at the chip level.
4. **Cost board** — mandate item 4, after live pass so it does not distract from empty cards.
5. **Discovery lane subagents** only if admit quality plateaus — mandate item 1 applied to breadth, not contact writes.

## Rejected interpretations of the mandate

- “More agents” as a goal by itself.
- Parallel contact specialists (phone agent, email agent) on one entity.
- Scripted dig to chase scoreboard.
- Claiming architectural completion without live integrity-ok scoreboard.

## How to continue the plan

Write **ADR-depth** volumes (vol 269 standard) that cite this mandate. Prefer extending 264–274 themes over new one-page checklists. Implementation work prioritizes the live scoreboard gate (vol 255) before new topology.
