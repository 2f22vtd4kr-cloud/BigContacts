# Volume 04 — Discovery vs Dig

**Suite:** APEX_ATLAS_MASTER_BUREAU_PLAN

---

## 1. Two brains, one product

| Mode | Goal | Freedom |
|------|------|---------|
| **Discovery** | Put **candidate people** on the ledger with provenance (registries, categories, capital relevance) | May use broader automation and market/category seeds |
| **Dig** | For a **chosen target**, free multi-tool research → contact routes | Full free ReAct; models decide tools |

### 1.1 Separation rules

1. Discovery must not consume the entire dig budget on one playbook.  
2. Dig must not re-run the entire discovery map as a scripted force list.  
3. Operator-facing progress must **name the mode** (registry discovery vs dig on Name).  
4. Card promotion rules apply to dig results; discovery inserts still require non-synthetic identity.

---

## 2. Discovery notes

- EDGAR / CH / BRREG / BODACC-class sources admit real public people—not demo rows.  
- Deterministic EDGAR page-0 + fixed term order caused “same first HNWI every Launch”; shuffle terms/offsets/harvester order where still relevant.  
- Name dedupe (normalized, middle initials) at insert to avoid triple cards.  
- Deceased / obviously stale targets should not remain “live outreach” cards after probe.

---

## 3. Dig notes

- Depth profiles: `RESEARCH_DEPTH=fast|standard|deep` must actually change iteration/action caps (no silent clip under a lower absolute cap).  
- Standard/deep for head-to-head vs single agent; fast is bulk-cheap.  
- Secondary dig paths (orchestrator after discovery) must pass `jobId` into live events so Reactor sees tool steps.

---

## 4. Phase messaging

Single normalizer for operator strings, e.g.:

- `Registry 7/9 · EDGAR/CH…`  
- `Dig · Robert W Philip · web research`  
- `Phase 8/10 · domain resolution`  

Ban simultaneous contradictory `[8/9] 🍳` and `Phase 8/10 J4–J9` without reconciliation.

---

## 5. Acceptance tests

1. Discovery-only smoke can insert people without requiring full dig.  
2. Dig-only / singleTargetId re-cook promotes without rediscovery.  
3. Progress message mode matches actual orchestrator branch.  
4. No fixed “Window N of M planned dig steps” copy.

---

## 6. Handoff to Volume 05

Volume 05 specifies **Reactor observability** so operators can see free dig and trust idle/live state.
