# Volume 168 — Public Email Recovery (No Paid Graph Dependency)

## Principle

Emails on the card must be **source-backed**. Pattern guessing alone is not promote-grade without a public observation that supports the pattern or the exact address.

## Public recovery methods (dig may use via search/visit)

1. **Exact address on page** — team, contact, press, IR, PDF, filing
2. **Domain harvest** — mailto: on official hosts
3. **SERP operators** — `"Name" "@domain"`, `site:domain filetype:pdf`, press mentions
4. **Pattern support** — once a real `@domain` is observed on a public page, pattern evidence can inform **candidates** in the evidence bag; promote exact addresses with URLs first
5. **GitHub/public commit emails** on company domain — observation only; collision-aware

## Forbidden as product core

- Breach corpora
- Purchased lead DBs as primary source
- Promoting pure guess without sourceUrl

## Card marks

- Personal professional email with attribution → personal / direct candidate
- Role inboxes → organization
- Never hide role inboxes when they are the best public path

## Acceptance

Fixtures where baseline chat finds `ir@` or press@ must not show empty email **and** empty phone on Apex if those pages were reachable.
