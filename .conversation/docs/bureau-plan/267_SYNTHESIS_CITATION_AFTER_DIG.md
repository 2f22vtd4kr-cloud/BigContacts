# Volume 267 — Synthesis and Citation After Dig

## Anthropic citation pass

Anthropic’s Research system often separates **finding** from **attribution**: workers gather material; a later pass ensures claims map to sources. Apex promote is the closest analogue — but promote is **workflow policy**, not a free-form prose synthesizer.

## Apex claim lifecycle

1. **Observe** — dig tool results (snippets, pages).
2. **Extract** — candidate vectors with source URLs in findings.
3. **Persist evidence** — `contact_evidence` bag (durable).
4. **Promote** — card columns under source locks and Personal vs Org rules.
5. **Present** — ContactSurface + evidence panel (operator-facing citation).

Skipping (3) and writing only columns loses rehydrate and audit. Skipping URLs fails citation honesty.

## Synthesis responsibilities

| Actor | Synthesizes |
|-------|-------------|
| Dig agent | When to stop; which findings are identity-fit |
| Promote workflow | Which vector wins the column; outcome enum |
| Right-hand / Live Desk (optional) | Human-readable story; must not override promote |
| Scoreboard rubric | Analytic score −1…2; not a contact inventor |

## Failure modes

- **Prose without vectors** — narrates “likely reachable” with no route.
- **Vectors without URLs** — unattributable; should not become confident Personal.
- **Org labeled Personal** — integrity and scoreboard damage.
- **Chat COMPARE without sources** — invalid baseline (vol 250).

## Plan implication

Post-scoreboard investment should prefer **citation UX** (every chip shows source) over new agents. Vol 243 remains the detailed citation layer plan; this volume binds it to the completed dig path.
