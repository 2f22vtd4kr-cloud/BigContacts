# Volume 166 — Maximum Public Surface (Never Eliminate Contacts)

## Product law

**Recover and show the maximum set of attributable public contact routes.** Prefer personal. Never discard org, IR, notice, transfer-agent, press, or related routes that a competent human or chat agent would keep on the table.

“Eliminating contacts” because they are not personal mobiles is a **product failure**.

## Public surface map (exhaustive for dig orientation)

### A. Issuer / company

- EDGAR registrant telephone and address (10-K/10-Q header)
- IR contact pages (`/investor`, `/ir`, Contact IR) — phones and `ir@` / `investors@` emails
- Outsourced IR firms (common: named principals + direct lines on IR agency pages)
- Press / media contact blocks
- Transfer agent phones (shareholder services — still a human path for public companies)
- Company Contact / About / Team pages
- PDF press kits and annual reports (filetype:pdf)

### B. Person-linked filings

- SC 13D/G notice phones and “copy to” counsel
- DEF 14A director/officer tables; ages; sometimes city
- Form 3/4 footnotes and addresses when public
- Proxy “attention” lines

### C. Professional web

- Public LinkedIn `/in/` (role confirmation + message path)
- Conference speaker pages, podcast show notes
- University / foundation bios tied to known roles
- GitHub public commits on company domains (email pattern evidence — **pattern support**, not invented inboxes without source)

### D. Domain & registry

- WHOIS/RDAP admin/tech (often privacy-proxied — still record domain)
- Companies House / BRREG officers and registered office phones when present
- Corporate registries in target jurisdiction

### E. Graph

- Same-issuer peers and officers (related strip)
- IR agency contacts as **organization** routes for the issuer context

## Display law (again)

Every recovered non-trash route appears in:

- Primary if best, else
- Chip row / contacts[] with mark, else
- Evidence drawer with count badge — **never silent delete**

## Dig behavior

The model is steered by **orientation** toward this surface map, not by force_* scripts. Observations should make IR pages, sec.gov hits, and contact blocks obvious. Budgets (standard/deep) exist to **spend more search/visit**, not to rationalize empty cards.

## Anti-patterns (banned)

- “Privacy-hardened → skip dig depth”
- Dropping IR phone because outcome ≠ direct personal
- Promote rules that only keep personal and trash org
- UI that hides org chips
- Scoreboard that scores “empty” as success when baseline had IR

## Acceptance

On any public-company-linked fixture: if a human can find IR or registrant phone in five minutes on the open web, Apex card after standard dig must show **at least one** phone or email route with source — or an explicit dig failure (L-NO-DIG / L-PROMOTE), never a quiet empty “done.”
