# Volume 173 — Dig Orientation: Surface Map Language

## Purpose

Give the free dig model a **mental map** of where public contacts live, without scripting actions.

## Orientation paragraphs (ship-ready intent)

You are recovering **public outreach routes** for a person, preferably personal phone/email, but **organization and IR routes are valuable and must be reported** when found.

When a company or issuer is known, public investor relations pages, SEC filings, company contact pages, and press contacts often publish phones and emails. Visit those pages when search results point to them. Report every attributable phone and email with the exact source URL. Do not omit IR or office lines because they are not mobile numbers. Do not invent addresses. Prefer findings with clear person or role attribution.

## What not to put in orientation

- Numbered forced steps
- “Always visit result #1”
- “Stop if no personal mobile”
- “Skip org contacts”

## Maintenance

Update when new high-yield public surfaces appear (new IR CMS patterns, regional registries). Keep short; long orientation competes with observations for context.
