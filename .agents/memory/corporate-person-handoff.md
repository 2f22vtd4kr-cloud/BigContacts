---
name: Corporation-to-person review handoff
description: Named people found while researching corporations must survive as linked review candidates without becoming HNWIs or contacts.
---

Organization research can produce valuable directors, officers, auditors, operators, or owners even when it finds no validated personal contact vector. Persist those people separately from contact discovery with the company relationship, role, evidence basis, jurisdiction, and source URLs.

**Why:** A contact-gated persistence branch previously discarded the useful corporation-to-person result when no email or phone was found. That made the profile appear empty even though research had surfaced named principals.

**How to apply:** Keep candidates review-only and explicitly non-HNWI until exact identity, ownership attribution, and reachability are independently adjudicated. Do not infer Swedish officer authority from Companies House; use a jurisdiction-appropriate source.
