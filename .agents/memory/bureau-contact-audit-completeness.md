---
name: Bureau contact-audit completeness
description: Durable rule for distinguishing candidate discovery from verified public contact-route coverage.
---

Discovery candidate names, summaries, and source URLs are not evidence that a contact route was found. A Bureau pass is contact-complete only when every persisted candidate is explicitly audited for named decision-makers, public professional profiles, organization routes, and any directly sourced email or phone route; candidates without qualifying evidence must carry an explicit empty result.

**Why:** An earlier discovery case had many useful names and citations but no candidate-level contact structure, making it impossible to tell whether contacts were found or merely omitted from the report.

**How to apply:** Keep contact evidence review-only, preserve the exact value and source URL, label the scope and attribution, and show “no structured contact evidence” separately from organization/social routes in the UI.
