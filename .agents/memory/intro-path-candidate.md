name: Intro Path Candidate
description: Review-only intermediary route derived from durable evidence for practical investor introductions
---

The practical access layer may derive at most one **Intro Path Candidate** per target from existing durable contact evidence. It is separate from direct personal contact promotion.

**Why:** The useful outcome is a credible, evidence-backed introduction path, but organization routes and named-person candidates are not automatically personal contacts or authorization to reach out.

**How to apply:** Require an exact public source URL, preserve scope and role, accept organization routes or named professional intermediary roles only, show the candidate as `review_required`, and require manual identity/affiliation/appropriateness verification. Never write it to personal email, phone, social fields, contact outcome, hot status, or generate outbound copy automatically.
