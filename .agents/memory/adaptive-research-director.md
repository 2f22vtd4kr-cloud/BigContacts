---
name: Adaptive research director
description: The bounded, evidence-gap-driven controller that coordinates canonical web research without weakening publication gates.
---

Atlas's adaptive behavior belongs as a target-scoped pre-pass inside the canonical `web-enricher.ts` path, not as a replacement for the existing extractor or as a second incompatible research-plan schema.

**Why:** The fixed provider fan-out could find citations without deciding what to investigate next, while a fully autonomous loop would risk repeated searches, page noise, and premature contact promotion. The controller adds re-planning while preserving the existing evidence ledger and fail-closed adjudication.

**How to apply:** Keep the director bounded and stateful. Let identity, operator/parent, official-domain, named-person, exact-claim, and provider-lane gaps select the next action. Feed discovered domains and people into exact-page retrieval, persist only a compact action trace, and never treat adaptive provider citations or contacts as publishable without the existing canonical URL, attribution, corroboration, and reachability checks.
