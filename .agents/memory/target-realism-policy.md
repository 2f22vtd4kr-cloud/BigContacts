---
name: Target realism policy
description: Durable rules for separating research evidence from legitimate public access routes.
---

Research reachability must be classified before expensive retrieval, graph exploration, or outreach generation. A direct route requires an attributable public direct vector; an intermediary requires both an attributable public vector and corroborated relationship evidence. Wealth, fame, press coverage, social handles, assets, registries, switchboards, generic inboxes, brokers, property staff, and hypothetical assistants are evidence only. Isolated prominent targets should receive a research-only review state and no speculative outreach.

**Why:** Public-figure research can produce abundant signals while still having no realistic route to contact; treating those signals as access wastes compute and creates misleading outreach.

**How to apply:** Keep the policy deterministic, persist the classification with research sessions, pass it into provider prompts, prevent MCTS assets from becoming gatekeepers, and make pitch generation refuse bounded or research-only sessions.
