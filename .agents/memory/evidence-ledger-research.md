---
name: Evidence-led research scoring
description: Rules for preventing provider agreement and global citations from being mistaken for independent source confirmation.
---

Use source URLs and publisher domains as the unit of corroboration, not the number of AI/search providers that repeat a value. Search and AI outputs are discovery signals; page-level or registry evidence is needed before treating a contact or ownership claim as strongly confirmed. A search engine's separate result-URL list is not claim-level provenance unless the specific result is fetched and the value is observed there.

**Why:** Multiple providers can read the same indexed page, and assigning a global citation list to every named person creates false attribution.

**How to apply:** Keep claim-level source URLs, canonicalize and deduplicate mirrored pages, cap AI-only and flattened-snippet confidence, and retain unsupported person/contact findings as review candidates.
