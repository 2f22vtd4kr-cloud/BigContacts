---
name: Blind review truth boundary
description: Acceptance rule for target-scoped research publication and audit results
---

The final blind-review verdict is only as trustworthy as its deterministic approved-value set and scope classification. A reviewer narrative or terminal publish status must not be treated as approval when the approved values are empty, the source is organization-scoped, or identity collision risk remains.

**Why:** A live Andy Cantwell proof run reached `publish` and `contact_route_found`, but the reviewer’s approved-contact list was empty while its prose claimed a personal email approval. Treating prose as truth would have promoted an unsupported personal route.

**How to apply:** For acceptance audits, compare the final card, approved values, evidence scope, source URL, and reviewer reasons. Any contradiction is a quality failure even if the job completes without errors.
