---
name: Manual research honesty
description: Distinguish an assistant's ad hoc web investigation from the application's full evidence pipeline.
---

Manual web research must state which lanes were actually searched, which pages were fetched, and which claims remain unresolved. It must not be presented as equivalent to a complete Atlas run unless the application's identity, attribution, contradiction, provenance, and route-ranking stages actually ran.

**Why:** A hand-picked source pass can reach the right direction while still lacking the target fingerprint, negative search, independent corroboration, or server-side contact adjudication required by the product contract.

**How to apply:** Report manual findings as source-linked leads or an evidence ledger, disclose inaccessible pages and unrun stages, and use the stricter fail-closed labels for ownership, executive, intermediary, organization, and unresolved routes.
