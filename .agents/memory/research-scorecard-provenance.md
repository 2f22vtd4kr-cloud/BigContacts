---
name: Research scorecard provenance
description: Independent research scoring must consume canonical evidence and attribution rather than registry or provider counts
---

Research scorecard categories are intentionally separate. Identity and ownership require corroborated canonical evidence and attribution; contact requires validated direct-route evidence; access comes only from practical reachability; wealth remains a separate signal; freshness and source quality use dated evidence and source reliability.

**Why:** Asset counts, graph degree, registry labels, provider repetition, and high wealth scores previously made sparse or unreachable targets appear more certain and actionable than the evidence supported.

**How to apply:** Keep candidate funnel states and canonical source domains available at the scorecard call site. Treat missing provenance as low/review-only, and recompute from stable evidence on every rerun rather than feeding prior posterior scores back into research.
