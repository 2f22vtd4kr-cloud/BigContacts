---
name: Evidence-led entity cards
description: Durable UX rule for HNWI, organization, and profile cards
---

Entity cards are the primary Apex Atlas surface and should lead with a readable public-profile brief and an involvement summary, not only scores and contact fields. Narrative copy must come from stored headlines, social bios, foundation records, registry activity, notes, or linked assets.

**Why:** A wealth or reachability score alone does not tell the researcher who the person is or what the public record connects them to. Inferred biographies or guessed involvement would violate the project's evidence-first data-quality standard.

**How to apply:** Keep the same evidence-led narrative treatment across dashboard priority cards, the People ledger on desktop/mobile, and the profile hero. When evidence is absent, say it is not recorded rather than generating a plausible-sounding description.
