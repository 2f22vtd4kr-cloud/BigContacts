name: Campione benchmark
description: First controlled Atlas-versus-independent comparison on an Italian casino legal entity.
---

On 2026-08-06, a clean single-target Atlas retry for Casinò di Campione S.p.A. completed with zero run errors but produced no promoted route, five page-noise person candidates, and eleven review-only contact-evidence rows, most unrelated organization signals. An independent search found official leadership pages identifying Stefano Silvestri and a named business email.

**Why:** Wider provider fan-out did not beat a focused exact-domain search on this target; provider/domain drift and page-noise consumed the evidence budget before official leadership claims were promoted.

**How to apply:** Benchmark both discovery breadth and exact official-domain retrieval. Count named business routes separately from personal direct routes, and treat unrelated-domain evidence as a quality failure even when retained review-only.
