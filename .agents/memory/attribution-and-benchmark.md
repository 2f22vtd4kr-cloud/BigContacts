The valid benchmark is a controlled cohort of 16 less-famous, business-linked FAA aircraft owners, not a celebrity such as Warren Buffett. The clean enrichment run completed 16/16 with 0 errors: 13 social-only, 1 direct-contact candidate, and 2 with no usable contact outcome.

**Why:** Celebrity benchmarks make public reachability look artificially strong. The FAA cohort measures the real problem: identity and access for wealthy but less-famous individuals.

**How to apply:** Keep organization accounts, same-name/public-figure candidates, AI-only citations, and provider agreement without exact canonical claim URLs in durable review evidence only. HNWI social promotion and Maigret/Sherlock pivots require a current-run candidate with target-person attribution and an exact fetched claim URL; never fall back to legacy entity handles. Treat 1/16 direct-contact yield as a measured limitation, not a reason to inflate scoring.
