---
name: Yield widening
description: Discovery budgets should expand before adjudication while evidence promotion remains fail-closed.
---

Atlas should widen candidate and source-page retention before changing any evidence or contact-promotion rule. Named people, operator pages, provider citations, and exact claim URLs are useful only if they survive long enough to reach the existing adjudication pipeline.

**Why:** Narrow handoff caps can make a multi-provider run look empty by discarding valid owners, executives, operators, and intermediary routes before provenance and identity gates can evaluate them.

**How to apply:** Increase bounded discovery/follow-up budgets and preserve role/provenance context, then measure useful yield with people discovered, resolutions, evidence rows, candidate routes, verified direct routes, review routes, and source-domain diversity. Do not loosen identity, attribution, exact-page, generic-mailbox, or reachability gates.
