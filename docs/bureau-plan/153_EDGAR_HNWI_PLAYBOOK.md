# Volume 153 — EDGAR HNWI Playbook (Public Company / Filer Paths)

## Why EDGAR remains central

For US public-company-linked wealth, EDGAR is the highest-trust **public** source of:

- Registrant telephone and address (10-K/10-Q headers),
- Beneficial owners and group members (SC 13D/G),
- Officers/directors and sometimes ages/addresses (DEF 14A, Form 3/4),
- Notice-related contact patterns on schedules.

Commercial executive-contact playbooks in 2026 still prescribe EDGAR first for public companies, then company site, then email patterns — and warn that filings yield **routing**, not guaranteed personal mobiles. Apex must encode that honesty on the card.

## Model-facing orientation (not a forced script)

Teach via orientation, not force_*:

- Prefer queries that combine **person name + issuer + form type**.
- When SERP shows `sec.gov`, visiting is high value.
- Notice phones ≠ generic 1-800 issuer lines; label sources distinctly.

## Extraction priorities from visited filings

1. tel: / telephone patterns near “name,” “copy to,” “attention,” “notice”
2. Email patterns on notice pages
3. Related person names in group filings
4. Street addresses for residence vs business discrimination

## Promote rules (recap)

- Notice-class → protect like agentic personal for overwrite purposes
- Registrant tel → organization_contact, still on card
- Never promote collision hosts as personal

## Acceptance fixtures

At least two fixtures in the scoreboard set must be EDGAR-grounded (known filer + known notice or registrant line in baseline).
