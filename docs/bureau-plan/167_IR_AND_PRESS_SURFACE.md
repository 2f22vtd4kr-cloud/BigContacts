# Volume 167 — IR, Press, and Transfer-Agent Surface

## Why this volume exists

Investor relations and press contacts are among the **most reliable public phones/emails** for public companies. They are staffed. They are published deliberately. Chat agents surface them constantly. Apex must treat them as **first-class organization routes**, not noise.

## Patterns observed in the wild (2025–2026)

- Dedicated IR microsites: phones + `IR@` / `investors@` inboxes
- Outsourced IR shops listing **named** outreach staff with direct dials
- Transfer agents (AST, Equiniti, etc.) with toll-free and international lines
- Media contact blocks on newsrooms

All are **valid outreach or routing paths**. Label as organization (or named person if the IR staffer is the finding and attributed).

## Model orientation snippets (examples, not forced queries)

- When issuer known: look for investor relations contact pages and visit them.
- When SERP shows IR agency + issuer: visit both.
- Prefer recording both phone and email from the same IR block.

## Promote / present

- `ir@`, `investors@`, `media@`, `press@` → organization mark, still on card
- Named IR staffer with direct phone → can be personal mark **for that staffer entity**, or org route on the issuer-linked HNWI card with clear label “IR contact”

## Never

- Filter out toll-free as “useless” without checking if it is the only public line
- Confuse transfer-agent lines with the target’s mobile — label correctly, still show
