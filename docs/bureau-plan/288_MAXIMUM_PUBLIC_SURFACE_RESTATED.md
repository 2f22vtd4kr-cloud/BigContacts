# Volume 288 — Maximum Public Surface (Restated)

## Product law

For outreach-oriented HNWI / principal research:

- **Personal routes preferred** when public and attributable.
- **Organization, IR, notice-line, press, counsel, and related-staff routes are kept and shown**—never dropped to “look cleaner.”
- **Empty card = bug** when DigSpan shows visits that extracted facts or evidence rows exist.

## Explicit rejection

“Privacy-hardened empty cards” as a quality story is rejected. Privacy constraints may limit *illegal* or *non-public* collection; they do not justify hiding public IR phones or SEC notice lines.

## UI implication

ContactSurface and profile REACH list **all** presented contacts with scope labels. Operators decide what to use; the desk does not pre-delete org surface.
