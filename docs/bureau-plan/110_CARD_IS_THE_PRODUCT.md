# Volume 110 — Card Is the Product

**Part of:** APEX_ATLAS_MASTER_BUREAU_PLAN

Logs, spans, and schemes are for operators and debugging.  
The **entity card** (outcome, phone, email, sources, notes) is what must beat a single agent’s answer.

Priority order for engineering:

1. Free dig produces real findings  
2. Findings promote to card honestly  
3. Spans show the path  
4. UI polish  

Never optimize 3–4 while 1–2 lose scoreboards.
