# Volume 179 — Why Organization Contacts Are Not Second-Class

## The false hierarchy

Apex historically treated “organization_contact” as a soft failure state — something to skip, hide, or overwrite while hunting a mythical personal mobile. That hierarchy **eliminates the majority of real public outreach paths** for issuer-linked wealth.

## What org contacts actually do for outreach

1. **Human on the line** — IR desks, main switchboards, and transfer agents answer.
2. **Routing** — “Please connect me to the office of …” is a normal professional pattern.
3. **Documentation** — filings and IR pages are **meant** to be used; using them is not a dirty workaround.
4. **Parity with chat** — single-LLM sessions proudly return IR phones; Apex looked broken when it hid them.
5. **Graph seeds** — org domain and phone support the next dig on related people.

## Labeling is enough

The fix is not to pretend org phones are personal. The fix is:

- Honest mark + outcome
- Equal visibility on the card
- Promote protection so dig-found org lines are not wiped by junk

## Scoreboard ethics

Awarding score 1 for correct org recovery is **success**, not a consolation prize. Awarding 0 when org was available and missed is **failure**.

## Product copy

Prefer “Public routes” and “Organization route — validate before outreach” over language that shames org recovery.
