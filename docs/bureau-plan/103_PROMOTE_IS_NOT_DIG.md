# Volume 103 — Promote Rules Are Not Dig Scripts

**Part of:** APEX_ATLAS_MASTER_BUREAU_PLAN

Operators sometimes hear “rules” and fear model freedom is dying. Split clearly:

| Layer | Freedom | Rules |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Dig | **Full** model choice of tools | Only budgets and honest observations |
| Promote | **No** model invention of phone strings | Fail-closed TypeScript: sanitize, collision, source priority, outcome |

Promote rules exist because **models must not invent** card fields. Dig freedom exists because **models must invent the research path**.

Scoreboard losses from empty cards are promote bugs.  
Scoreboard losses from scripted dig are dig-freedom bugs.  
Do not “fix” empty cards by scripting dig.
