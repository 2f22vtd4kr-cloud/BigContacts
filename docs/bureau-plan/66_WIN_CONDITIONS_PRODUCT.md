# Volume 66 — Product Win Conditions (Definition of Done for “Best in Class”)

**Part of:** APEX_ATLAS_MASTER_BUREAU_PLAN

Apex is **best in class** for this product only when all of the following hold on a fixed public fixture set, integrity ok, depth standard/deep:

1. **Free dig process** — majority of digs show model-invented queries and at least one primary visit when SERP provides one  
2. **Card dissemination** — empty-after-dig rate near zero when URL-backed facts exist and collision allows  
3. **Honesty** — no systematic direct_* on issuer/generic org routes  
4. **Identity** — collision classes do not produce wrong-person directs  
5. **Comparison** — independent open primary-source audit does not systematically beat Apex on firm lines and labels  
6. **Operability** — Launch/Stop/status reliable; idle is idle  
7. **No script regression** — force_* controllers absent on healthy path  

Until then: architecture may be right; **product is not done**.

### Anti-done

- Beautiful Launch button  
- 1700+ commits  
- Long plan corpus without scoreboard  
- Registry-only phase completion without dig promote
