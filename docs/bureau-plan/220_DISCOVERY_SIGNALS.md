# Volume 220 — Public Signals That Make a Good Target

## High-value signals (public)

| Signal | Why dig will work |
|--------|-------------------|
| SC 13D/G reporting person | Filings, notice paths, issuer context |
| Officer/director on public co | IR, proxy, company site |
| Form 4 insider | Identity + issuer binding |
| Named FO principal in press **with vehicle** | Searchable entity + person |
| RIA / ADV related individuals | Regulatory public footprint |
| Registry director + live company website | CH/BRREG + contact page |
| Gatekeeper named in filing “copy to” | Professional route |

## Weak signals (admit sparingly)

- “Richest people in X” listicles
- Single social handle, no org
- Yacht/jet aesthetic with no owner attribution
- Trademark “family office” with no people

## Industry practice (2025–2026) — mapped

OSINT allocator intel emphasizes **filings, board seats, press, job posts, foundations** — continuous public signals, not static wealth lists. Apex discovery should chase **signals that resolve to named people**, then dig for routes.

## Explicitly out of discovery core

Paid FO databases as required dependency. Optional later enrichers only.
