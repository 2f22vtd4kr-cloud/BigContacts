The Orient Express brand is owned and operated as a joint venture between the French hospitality giant Accor and the luxury conglomerate LVMH (Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton).Accor originally bought full control of the historic brand name in 2022. In 2024, LVMH acquired a 50% strategic stake in the brand, the hotels, the upcoming trains, and the entity that owns the ultra-luxury sailing yachts.The assets tied to this luxury venture break down across the sea, rails, and land:🌊 The Massive Sailing Yacht: Orient Express CorinthianThe massive sailing ship you are referring to is the Orient Express Corinthian, which was officially launched in mid-2026.The Scale: It is recognized as the largest sailing yacht in the world, stretching nearly 720 feet (220 meters) long. It features three massive masts reaching 328 feet high with automated rigid fiberglass/carbon "SolidSail" technology.The Luxury: Despite its massive footprint, it is designed for extreme exclusivity with only 54 ultra-luxury suites, a cabaret, an amphitheater, and multiple pools.Ownership Structure: The physical boat is owned by the subsidiary Orient Express Sailing Yacht, whose capital is split primarily between Accor and LVMH, alongside Swiss investment firm Millenium. A sister ship, the Orient Express Olympian, is scheduled to launch in 2027.🚂 The Luxury TrainsThe train asset ecosystem is split into two prominent branches:The "New" Orient Express Trains: Under their joint venture, Accor and LVMH launched the Orient Express La Dolce Vita train in 2025 (touring Italy) and are preparing to relaunch the historic, fully renovated Paris-to-Istanbul Orient Express train route in 2027.The Existing Venice Simplon-Orient-Express: LVMH independently owns Belmond, a hospitality company it acquired in 2019. Belmond owns and operates the iconic Venice Simplon-Orient-Express train. While it uses the "Orient Express" name via historic licensing, it remains a separate operations entity from the Accor joint-venture trains.🏨 Luxury HotelsThe joint venture has aggressively expanded the brand name into land-bound luxury real estate.
