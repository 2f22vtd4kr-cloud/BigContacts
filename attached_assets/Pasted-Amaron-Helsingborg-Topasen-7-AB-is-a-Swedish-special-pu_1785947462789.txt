Amaron Helsingborg Topasen 7 AB is a Swedish special-purpose holding company created by the real estate fund manager Amaron to hold its recently acquired commercial property Helsingborg Topasen 7. Because it is a shell entity with zero employees, bypassing the "front desk" means pitching directly to the executive leadership team of the parent fund, Amaron Fund Management AB, who hold the ultimate decision-making power. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

* Role: CEO of Amaron Real Estate AB and Fund Manager. He explicitly manages the commercial property funds and oversees the acquisition, development, and portfolio strategy for the Helsingborg assets.
   * Direct Contact Route: You can reach his fund management desk directly by routing through the executive registry at martin.mildner@amaron.se or via his fund office portal on the [Amaron Official Website](https://www.amaron.se/). [5, 6, 7, 8]

## Corporate Structure Overview
To tailor your offer accurately, keep in mind how this specific entity is structured within the larger organization:

| Entity Level | Entity Name | Function for Topasen 7AB |
|---|---|---|
| Asset Level | Amaron Helsingborg Topasen 7 AB | The specific property holding company. |
| Subsidiary Level | Amaron Real Estate AB | The operational arm managed by Martin Mildner[](https://www.google.com/search?q=martin+mildner&kgmid=/g/11fy2dn5v4). |
| Fund Level | Amaron Commercial Properties AB (publ) | The parent real estate fund listed on the Nordic Growth Market. |
