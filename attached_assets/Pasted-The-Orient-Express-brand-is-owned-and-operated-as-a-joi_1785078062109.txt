The Orient Express brand is owned and operated as a joint venture between the French hospitality giant Accor and the luxury conglomerate LVMH (Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton). [1, 2] 
Accor originally bought full control of the historic brand name in 2022. In 2024, LVMH acquired a 50% strategic stakein the brand, the hotels, the upcoming trains, and the entity that owns the ultra-luxury sailing yachts. [3, 4, 5] 
The assets tied to this luxury venture break down across the sea, rails, and land:
🌊 The Massive Sailing Yacht: Orient Express Corinthian
The massive sailing ship you are referring to is the Orient Express Corinthian, which was officially launched in mid-2026.[6, 7, 8, 9] 

* 
* The Scale: It is recognized as the largest sailing yacht in the world, stretching nearly 720 feet (220 meters) long. It features three massive masts reaching 328 feet high with automated rigid fiberglass/carbon "SolidSail" technology. [6, 10, 11, 12, 13] 
* The Luxury: Despite its massive footprint, it is designed for extreme exclusivity with only 54 ultra-luxury suites, a cabaret, an amphitheater, and multiple pools. [6, 12, 14] 
* Ownership Structure: The physical boat is owned by the subsidiary Orient Express Sailing Yacht, whose capital is split primarily between Accor and LVMH, alongside Swiss investment firm Millenium. A sister ship, the Orient Express Olympian, is scheduled to launch in 2027. [14, 15, 16, 17] 
* 
🚂 The Luxury Trains
The train asset ecosystem is split into two prominent branches:

* 
* The "New" Orient Express Trains: Under their joint venture, Accor and LVMH launched the Orient Express La Dolce Vita train in 2025 (touring Italy) and are preparing to relaunch the historic, fully renovated Paris-to-Istanbul Orient Express train route in 2027. [3, 18] 
* The Existing Venice Simplon-Orient-Express: LVMH independently owns Belmond, a hospitality company it acquired in 2019. Belmond owns and operates the iconic Venice Simplon-Orient-Express train. While it uses the "Orient Express" name via historic licensing, it remains a separate operations entity from the Accor joint-venture trains. [3, 4, 19, 20] 
* 
🏨 Luxury Hotels
The joint venture has aggressively expanded the brand name into land-bound luxury real estate. They debuted their first physical properties, including the Orient Express La Minerva in Rome and a property in Venice, to offer seamless train-to-hotel luxury itineraries. [3, 21, 22]
